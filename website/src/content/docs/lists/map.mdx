---
name: map
params: [list, transform]
arities: [2]
variadic: false
receives: list
returns: list
---

Apply a function to every element: `riff.map(up)`.
The transform is an ordinary user `fn` of one argument,
and may answer with anything — mapping to oscillators is the only way to hold several voices apart,
since a `for` over audio sums instead of collecting.
