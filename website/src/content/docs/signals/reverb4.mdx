---
name: reverb4
params: [audio, room_size, time]
arities: [3]
variadic: false
receives: signal
returns: signal
---

Reverb with a slow fade-in,
for swells rather than rooms.
`room_size` is in meters and is treated as at least 15; below that the delay times stop sounding like a space.
Wet only.
Both `room_size` and `time` are constants.
