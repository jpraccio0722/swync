returns: signal

Hybrid FDN reverb — richer and more expensive than `reverb`.
`room_size` is in meters and clamps to 10..=30,
`diffusion` in 0..=1 thickens the tail,
`modulation` around 1 adds movement (higher goes audibly Doppler),
and `damping_cutoff` is the lowpass applied to each loop pass,
in hertz.
Wet only.
All parameters except the audio input are constants.
