---
name: tap
params: [signal, delay, min_delay, max_delay]
arities: [4]
variadic: false
receives: signal
returns: signal
---

Tapped delay line with cubic interpolation.
Unlike `delay`,
the delay time is a signal,
so it can be modulated. It must stay within the constant `min_delay`..=`max_delay` bounds,
in seconds.
