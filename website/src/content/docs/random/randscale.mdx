---
name: randscale
params: [count, scale, lo, hi]
arities: [2, 4]
variadic: false
receives: number
returns: list
---

`count` notes drawn evenly from the tones of a scale given as semitone offsets within an octave between MIDI `lo` and `hi` (60..72 by default).
Unlike snapping a uniform draw with `scale`,
every degree is equally likely and nothing lands outside the range: `randscale(8, [0, 3, 5, 7, 10], 48, 72)` is a pentatonic riff over two octaves.
