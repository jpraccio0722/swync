---
name: slice
params: [buffer, start, end, rate, channel]
arities: [3, 4, 5]
variadic: false
receives: buffer
returns: signal
---

Read a portion of a buffer, once: `slice(amen, 0, 0.25)`.

`start` and `end` are between 0 and 1.

`start` past `end` plays that portion backwards.

`rate` defaults to 1.
