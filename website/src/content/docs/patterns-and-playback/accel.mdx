---
name: accel
params: [from, to, bars]
arities: [3]
variadic: false
receives: number
returns: rate
---

Accelerates a play's rate `from` to `to` over `bars` bars.
Holds at `to` upon completion.

Use in the rate parameter of `play` — `play(riff, lead, accel(1, 2, 4))`.

A `to` less than from `from` slows down — `play(riff, lead, accel(2, 1, 4))`
Both rates must be above zero: at zero the pattern would stop rather than slow.
