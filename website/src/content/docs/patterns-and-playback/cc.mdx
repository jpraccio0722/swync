---
name: cc
params: [device, number, channel, lo, hi]
arities: [2, 3, 5]
variadic: false
receives: number
returns: signal
---

One controller — a knob, a wheel, a pedal — as a signal: `cc("push", 74)` is
0 to 1. `channel` is 1-16 and defaults to 1.

Give a range to map it straight onto something: `cc("push", 74, 1, 200, 5000)`
is a filter cutoff.

Smoothed over a few milliseconds, because seven bits wired to a cutoff zippers.
Zero until the controller first moves, and silent when the port is not
connected.
