---
name: play
params: [pattern, instrument, rate]
arities: [2, 3]
variadic: false
receives: pattern
returns: play
---

Schedule a pattern on an instrument: `pat >> play(kick)`.
The instrument must name a user `fn`.

`rate` defaults to 1.

Any further function parameter is added as a lane — `play(bass, cut: [400, 2000])` 
lane's values are sampled at each note's onset.

Two names are reserved and reach the note rather than the instrument: `legato:` scales its length,
and `pan:` places it across the stereo field from -1 (left) through 0 (centre) to 1 (right).

All children of `play` follow the same conventions.
