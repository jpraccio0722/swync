---
name: quantize
params: [play, grid]
arities: [1, 2]
variadic: false
receives: play
returns: play
---

Round where a chain of plays has reached up to a multiple of `grid` bars,
without touching what is already playing: `playn(pat, inst, 3, 2).quantize().then(chorus)`.

`grid` defaults to 1.

Useful when a section's length is not a whole number of bars.
