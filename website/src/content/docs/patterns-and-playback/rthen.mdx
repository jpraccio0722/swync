---
name: rthen
params: [play, sections]
arities: [2]
variadic: false
receives: play
returns: play
---

`wthen` with every section equally likely: `playn(intro, lead, 2).rthen([verse, chorus, bridge])`. Rerolls each time round and never finishes, exactly as `wthen` does. Unlike `then`, the sections here must be `fn`s named rather than plays written out: this runs one afresh rather than placing it once.
